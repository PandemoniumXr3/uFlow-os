# uFlow — TestFlight Beta Tester Guide

Thanks for helping test uFlow. This should take about 15–20 minutes. You're testing an early
internal beta — things may be rough around the edges, and that's exactly what we want to find out.

## Before you start

- This is an early beta. **Please avoid entering real sensitive information** (real allergies you
  depend on for safety, real medical dietary restrictions, etc.) unless you're comfortable doing so
  — use placeholder/test data if you'd rather not. It's fine either way; this is your call.
- uFlow stores everything locally on your device only — there's no account and nothing is uploaded
  anywhere unless you explicitly export a backup or send feedback (see the in-app Settings for
  details).
- If the app crashes or looks badly broken at any point, you don't need to redo the whole route —
  just note where it happened and use the feedback flow described at the end.

## The test route (~15–20 minutes)

1. **Fresh launch.** Open uFlow for the first time (or after using "Clear all data" in Settings if
   you're re-testing).
2. **Onboarding.** Go through the setup flow. Try skipping a step if one is optional, and try
   entering a food preference (like an allergy or diet type) if prompted.
3. **Choose Quick Stock or demo setup.** Pick whichever option onboarding offers to get started —
   either add a few real Stock items or use the demo setup.
4. **Inspect Today.** Look at the Today screen — check that suggestions, your plan, and any Stock
   warnings look reasonable.
5. **Open a recipe.** Tap into any recipe's detail view.
6. **Change servings.** Adjust the servings count and confirm quantities/nutrition (if shown)
   scale sensibly.
7. **Plan a meal.** Add a meal to today's (or another day's) plan.
8. **Add a missing item to Grocery.** From a recipe or Today, add an ingredient you don't have in
   Stock to your Grocery list.
9. **Mark a Grocery item purchased.** Check it off in the Grocery tab.
10. **Verify Stock.** Confirm the purchased item now shows up in Stock.
11. **Export a backup.** Settings → Export data → export a full backup. Note where it saved/shared
    to.
12. **Change some data.** Add or remove a Stock item, plan another meal — anything that changes
    your data from what you just exported.
13. **Import the backup you made.** Settings → Import data → pick the file from step 11 → review
    the preview → choose Merge → confirm.
14. **Test undo.** Trigger one of the "Clear" actions in Settings (e.g. Clear meal plan) and tap
    "Undo" right away — confirm your data comes back.
15. **Submit feedback.** Settings → Beta feedback → send us anything — a bug, a suggestion, or just
    "this went fine."

## What to look for and report

Please tell us about:

- **Crashes** — what you were doing right before it happened.
- **Unclear wording** — anything confusing, ambiguous, or that made you unsure what would happen.
- **Wrong recommendations** — a suggested meal that clearly ignored an allergy/diet you set, or
  that made no sense given your Stock.
- **Data not refreshing** — a screen showing stale information after you changed something
  elsewhere.
- **Layout issues** — anything cut off, overlapping, or awkward on your specific device/screen size.
- **Actions that feel unsafe** — anything destructive that didn't warn you clearly enough, or that
  felt like it might lose data unexpectedly.
- **Anything showing zero/blank when data should exist** — e.g. a cost or nutrition value showing
  "0" instead of "not available" when it should be the latter.

## How to send feedback

**Settings → Beta feedback**, pick the category that fits best (bug / suggestion / general), and
send. This opens your mail app with some technical context already filled in (app version, device
platform, which screen you were on) — add your description and send. Nothing else is attached
automatically.

Thank you for testing!
